## 5. Monday refresh proposal: approval workflow

The Monday refresh is a proposal, not an automatic action. LeadLoop first presents the intended refresh in the app: review public, non-LinkedIn sources; add only supported concerns and questions; preserve human-entered work; and surface uncertain items for review. The proposal explicitly excludes LinkedIn login, scraping, posting, messaging, and outreach.

A human then chooses one of two actions:

1. **Approve proposal:** LeadLoop records that the refresh is approved for human review. In a production workflow, this approval would authorize the refresh process to prepare cited evidence and candidate idea rows for review; it would not authorize LinkedIn activity or publication.
2. **Not now:** LeadLoop keeps the proposal in queue for later. No refresh is applied.

Even after approval, evidence must remain separated into sourced facts, founder-reported experience, and agent interpretation. New ideas should contain neutral labels, direct sources where available, and 3–5 open questions. The **Written** and **Posted** statuses remain manual and human-owned. A person writes the post.
